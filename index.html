Inspired by the 2023 project Bioluminescent Fluid Simulation [1] and Taiwan's well-known firefly viewing events, which take place between April and May, we aim to simulate the flight trajectories and flashing patterns of fireflies in 3D space, along with interactions such as wind.

These behaviors have been simulated partially in several projects [5][6][7], but we have not seen an example that visualizes flashing and flight together with some interactions in 3D space. We would like to simulate a more realistic firefly scene based on prior studies, with improvements according to the biological characteristics of fireflies.
